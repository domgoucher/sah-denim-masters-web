Technical motorcycle clothing specialists

For more than a decade we've partnered with motorcycle brands as their technical apparel manufacturer of choice. Our seamsters combine generations of tailoring expertise with traditional construction methods to hand-make top-quality garments at highly competitive prices.

As a family business now in its second generation, we build long-term, stable relationships with both clients and suppliers.

Motorcycle clothing

Our focus on the increasingly complex world of motorcycle PPE has driven year-on-year growth in technical garment production. R&D into aramids and technical denims keeps our protective garments among the most highly rated on the market.

  • Aramid & protective garment development and testing expertise

  • CE-compliant garment manufacturing specialists
  • European project team — full support across the project life cycle
  • Ethical, socially compliant manufacturing facilities and practices
